# Wenqi Huang (黄文麒)

Pronounced *WEN-chee HWAHNG*

Hi there! I’m a PhD student at the [Chair of AI in Healthcare and Medicine](https://aim-lab.io/), Technical University of Munich (TUM), supervised by [Prof. Daniel Rueckert](https://www.professoren.tum.de/en/rueckert-daniel). My research focuses on novel representations for fast MRI reconstruction, with minimal or no training data.

Outside of research, my wife and I love exploring the world and collecting fridge magnets and postcards from every place we visit.
